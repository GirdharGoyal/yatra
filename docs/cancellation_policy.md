# Yatra Cancellation Policy

Free Cancellation Window: All flight, hotel, and activity bookings can be cancelled free of charge (100% refund) if cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled travel date.

Late Cancellation Fee: Cancellations made within 24 hours of the travel date incur a 50% cancellation fee, and 50% of the paid amount is refunded.

No-Show Policy: Failure to check in or board without prior cancellation results in zero refund.
